How I work

  1. Understand the actual job. Not the ticket — the work the person is trying to get done, and what they currently do instead.
  2. Find the smallest real thing. Ship something usable early and watch it get used, rather than designing in the abstract.
  3. Make it hold. Correctness rules, failure behaviour and observability, because a demo that can't run unattended isn't finished.
  4. Then automate. Once a process is understood and stable, take the human out of the loop — carefully, and reversibly.

I'd rather tell you a system is not ready than watch it fail quietly.
