What it is

The site is a working description of what I do: I take systems that are complicated, manual or half-abandoned — and make them something people can operate without a manual. Design work where the interface meets the infrastructure, and automation where the interface stops making sense.

It is hand-written. No framework, no build step, no third-party requests, no tracking. Four files and a font directory. It works with JavaScript disabled — everything the scripts do is enhancement, never load-bearing.

Three case studies are written up in full, including one where the first design failed on architecture rather than code, and what that changed.
