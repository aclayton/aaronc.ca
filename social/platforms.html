Platform-coloured marks

The mark in each platform's own brand colour, three ways: reversed out of a brand field, and drawn as the mark on his paper and night grounds.

A deliberate departure from the system. His palette is six neutrals and one rotating accent. These are third-party brand colours, used because the point is to sit beside a platform's chrome. They are experiments — nothing here belongs in style.css.

Brand hexes are sourced, not remembered: Simple Icons for most, LinkedIn from their own page. The reversed-mark colour is solved per brand against WCAG 1.4.11's 3:1 for a non-text graphic — which is what a mark is — not chosen by eye.

Second caveat: an avatar in a platform's exact brand colour can read as implying an affiliation. Worth a thought before it becomes the profile picture on a site that lists no employer.

The mark is read from logo.svg at build time; brand colours from simple-icons.json (3472 brands) and, for LinkedIn, their own published page. Contrast is WCAG relative luminance.

Rebuild with python3 build-platforms.py. Once a winner is picked, add it as a platform-<slug>.html render source and run it through render.sh + round-crop.py, the same pipeline the profile avatars use.
